How a project moves.

Four stages, in order, with one point of contact throughout. Start by sharing your city, project type, and rough scope.

I.

Consult

We begin with a call or video consultation. For projects nationwide, we confirm on-site walkthrough availability based on location, scope, and schedule.

II.

Design

Drawings, material palette, and a fixed proposal before anything is ordered.

III.

Build

On-site construction and installation are available nationwide, with location and schedule confirmed before the project begins. Clients receive weekly progress updates.

IV.

Care

Post-completion cleaning and an ongoing maintenance plan, if you want one.
